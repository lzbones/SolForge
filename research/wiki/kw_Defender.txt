==Description==
A creature with defender does not start a battle.

Creatures with Defender never attack although they do become offensive and can use [[Activate]] and [[Mobility]]. They can engage in battle and inflict damage to other creatures but they cannot attack players.

Certain cards like [[Flamerift Instigator]] can remove Defender from a creature and allow them to attack.

[[Energy Prison]] can add Defender to a creature, stopping it from attacking players.
