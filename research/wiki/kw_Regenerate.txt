==Description==
At the start of each turn, a creature with Regenerate X heals X damage from itself.

A creature cannot Regenerate health above their starting health stat. If a creature's health is increased with an effect such as [[Enrage]], they can regenerate up to their new starting health stat, which will be equal to their starting health plus the health gained from [[Enrage]].
