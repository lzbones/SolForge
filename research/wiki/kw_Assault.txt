==Description==
When this enters the field from your hand, it gets the ability listed after this keyword if there are no other creatures in its lane.

Note the "from your hand" clause in the keyword definition. If you reanimate an Assault creature with a card like {{cardtt|Varna's Pact}}, that creature's Assault ability will not trigger. Furthermore, if you use cards like {{cardtt|Stinging Invocation}} or {{cardtt|Phoenix Call}} with an {{cardtt|Ator, Thunder Titan}} in play, the creatures it creates will not have Aggressive.
