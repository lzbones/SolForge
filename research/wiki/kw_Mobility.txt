==Description==
While a creature with Mobility X is on the offensive, you can tap or click it to move it to one of your available spaces up to X lanes away once on each of your turns. 

The Mobility number indicates how many spaces the creature can move. A creature with {{Mobility|2}} may move up to two spaces in either direction. However, a creature can only move once per turn, regardless of how much Mobility it has. A creature cannot move into a lane if it is occupied
