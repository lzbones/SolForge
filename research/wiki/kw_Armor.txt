==Description==
A creature or player with Armor X prevents the first X damage dealt to it each turn.

Armor is an additional layer of protection that a creature or player can have in excess of its [[Health|health]]. Armor will neutralize the first damage done to a creature per turn, up to the number of Armor points the creature has. Any damage done in excess of the amount of Armor is inflicted on the creature's health.
